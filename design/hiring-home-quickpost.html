Design study · Hiring home

A place to post fast, and a clearer way to manage.

Same hiring home you have now — hero, live banner, stat strip, posted-gigs list all unchanged. Five ways to add a quick-post entry on the page and give Manage a real, discoverable treatment instead of a tiny top-right link.

Change 1
Quick post on the page

Posting shouldn't hide behind only the floating ＋. Give it a visible spot in the flow.

Change 2
Rework “Manage ›”

The 12-px link is easy to miss and vague. Make managing gigs an obvious, labelled action.

Variation 5
Hero button + manage footer

Quick post: a Post a gig button inside the hero — where your attention already is. Manage: a full-width Manage all gigs · 2 active › row closing the list.

My pick: Variation 2 (two buttons) is the clearest single move — one obvious Post, one obvious Manage, no hunting for a tiny link. If you want to keep the page shorter, Variation 5 spends no extra vertical space (post lives in the hero, manage closes the list). Variation 4's category chips are the most ambitious — great once posting-by-category is wired, but more to build.
