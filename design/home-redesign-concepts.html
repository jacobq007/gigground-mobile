GigGround · Home screen redesign

Hiring or working — pick one glance, not one guess

Three directions for separating "post a gig" from "apply to gigs" without locking anyone into a fixed account type. Interact with each phone: switch modes, tap the FAB, and watch how the layout — never the actions inside one menu — keeps the two intents apart.

A Persistent mode switch
Working / Hiring toggle
A segmented pill in the header swaps the entire home — hero, feed, stats, and the shared "Dashboard" tab (applications ⇄ posted gigs) — between two complete states. The FAB rebuilds itself per mode.
Tradeoff: total clarity per screen, at the cost of an extra tap whenever someone's actual week is genuinely half-and-half.

"What are you doing today?"
