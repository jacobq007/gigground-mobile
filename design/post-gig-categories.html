Design 5
Featured + more

The two commonest buckets get big rows; the rest sit in a compact 4-up below. Puts the likely choice one tap away while keeping everything visible.

My pick: Design 1 (two-step wizard). It matches the Skills & credentials page most closely — a clean grid, one clear job per screen — and the step bar makes the shorter form feel quick rather than empty. Wire the search from Design 4 into the details step's "Change" and you get inference for free. Design 3 (list rows) is the better call if you expect many categories; Design 5 if one or two categories will dominate real postings.
