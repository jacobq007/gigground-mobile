One list became two
Today SKILL_GROUPS in lib/skills.js is rendered by the worker’s Skills screen and the hirer’s Post screen alike. The two sides are asking different questions, so they need different nouns, different fields and different lifespans.
Skill category
WORKING SIDE
“What am I good at?”
A craft you own. Added once, levelled up over years, proved with a document. It outlives every job you take.
Sub-skills
Multi-select chips inside the category — Maths, Science, Coding
Level
New / Experienced / Expert, set per skill
Years
A stepper per skill, feeds ranking
Proof
Category-specific: degree, FSSAI, trade licence, portfolio. Unlocks the shield.
Tags
Boards you teach, meals you take, who you serve
Working mode
At their home / online / my place. Yours, not the job’s.
Travel radius
How far you will go for this category
Expected rate
What you want. Read by rateFit().
READ BY
Profile strength · verification tier · the CV screen · applicant compatibility
Keeps the six categories it already has. Nothing to migrate.
Need category
HIRING SIDE
“What do I need covered, and when?”
A shift with a headcount and a clock. It is created, filled, and then it stops existing. You never level it up — you repeat it.
Headcount
How many people. A worker never has this field.
Shift window
Date plus start and end time. Not “flexible”.
Requirement bar
Anyone reliable, or must be verified. This is what consumes the worker’s proof.
Must bring
Own bike, knife roll, FSSAI card. Per need, not per person.
Pay vs median
Guidance from real peers — already computed in HirerShift.jsx
Standby
Hold a second choice against a no-show
Repeat
Save as a weekly slot; refill from your bench in one tap
Arrival code
Four digits at the door. Already in codeFor().
READ BY
Fill state · the applicant pool · the standby slot · your bench
New. Shaped by BIZ_CATEGORIES, which is already in lib/constants.js and unused today.
